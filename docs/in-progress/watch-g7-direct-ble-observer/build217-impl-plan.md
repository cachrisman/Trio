### Task 1 — W-1 stage 2: raise the 2 s GATT command timeout (watch/background-gated) — ✅ GO (G1 passed: 58% starved-CPU)

- [ ] Raise `applyConfiguration(discoveryTimeout:)` (fork, `G7PeripheralManager.swift`, default 2 s) to ~6 s **only when `G7BackgroundHints.isHostBackgrounded`** (iPhone + foreground watch unchanged). G1 confirmed 58% of `command_timeout`s fire on a `.connected` peripheral (live link, late callback) — the starved-CPU population the raise targets. **Gate confirmed = backgrounded, not session-state** (open-Q2 split: these timeouts are all non-foreground but span both session states; a session-state gate would miss the 11 session-active-background cases).
- **Hypothesis:** on a background-starved watch, discovery/CCCD callbacks land just past 2 s; the timeout converts a live connection into `configure_block_skipped` retry churn inside the ~6–10 s transmitter window.
- **Change:** one constant threaded through the configure path, watch/background-gated; no control-flow change.
- **Pass/fail:** `command_timeout op=discover_services|set_notify_authentication` per 100 connects drops ≥50%; `gatt_ready → auth_notify_subscribed` conversion 84% → ≥92%; `egv_received`/`did_connect` does not regress. Kill-switch: revert the constant.
- **Gate outcome (G1, ✅ not killed):** the timeouts are **not** overwhelmingly dead-link — 58% fire on a `.connected` peripheral. The remaining ~42% (`peripheral_state != 2`) are dead-link and won't benefit from the raise, which is exactly why the change is background-gated and the pass bar is ≥50% (not 100%) reduction. (Kill criterion had been: overwhelmingly dead-link ⇒ don't build.)

### Task 2 — W-4: connect-watchdog escalation (stop the blind 60 s re-cancel loop) — ✅ GO (G2 cleared: age>200 s tail = 17%)

### Task 3 — W-3: gap-conditional backfill subscribe in background — ✅ CLEARED, LOW PRIORITY (G5: modest upside)
